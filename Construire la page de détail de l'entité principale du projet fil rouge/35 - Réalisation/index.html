About SoliCode Center

A pioneering center for training in the digital field

SoliCode Center is a specialized training center located in the heart of Tangier, Morocco. We offer comprehensive programs in web development, mobile app development, and other digital fields, and we prepare our trainees for the requirements of the modern job market.

Our mission is to provide high-quality, practical training that bridges the gap between education and professional life.

  • 💻 Modern equipment
  • 📋 Practical training
  • 🏆 Accredited programs
